# # AI STORY CREATOR — ROUGH ALGORITHM

# **Purpose:** Transform a simple user-provided story idea into a complete, well-structured story using a multi-step process.

# ### Workflow

# 1. **Analyze Input:** Identify the story idea, theme, characters, location, genre, and tone. Extract details provided by the user.
# 2. **Choose Theme & Title:** Determine the central theme and create a suitable title.
# 3. **Introduce Characters:** Define the main characters and their roles.
# 4. **Establish Setting:** Determine the location, time, and environment.
# 5. **Exposition:** Introduce the protagonist, background, and initial situation.
# 6. **Rising Action:** Build tension through obstacles, challenges, and conflicts.
# 7. **Climax:** Develop the turning point and highest moment of tension.
# 8. **Falling Action:** Show the consequences of the climax and gradually resolve conflicts.
# 9. **Resolution:** Resolve the main conflict and establish the new normal.

# **Core Rule:** Follow the steps sequentially, maintain consistency, and use the user's input as the foundation of the story.
